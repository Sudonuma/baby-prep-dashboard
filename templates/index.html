Safety note / sleep

Keep sleep separate from “cute layering.”

For sleep, think sleepsuit + an appropriate sleep bag, rather than adding cardigans, bulky jumpers or loose extra layers. Check the sleep-bag manufacturer's guidance for TOG and the room temperature. Remove hats indoors and in warm cars.

Sources

The layer logic and starter quantities are a planning framework, not a medical prescription. The NHS recommends about 1 extra layer in winter and notes that several light layers are preferable to one heavy layer; it also recommends keeping sleep clothing simple to avoid overheating.

NHS: “How to dress a newborn” • NHS: “What you’ll need for your baby” • Sanetta size guide
